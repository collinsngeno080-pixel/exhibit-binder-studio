How It Works

From raw documents to a court-ready binder.

Five steps. The system keeps pagination, numbering, Bates stamps, and bookmarks in sync so you don't have to.

The part of binder prep that breaks under deadline.

Today, most exhibit binders are still assembled largely by hand: renumbering pages in Acrobat, retyping the exhibit index in Word, and rebuilding bookmarks after every last-minute document swap.

Exhibit Binder Studio's core promise: pagination, exhibit numbering, Bates numbers, and bookmarks stay synchronized automatically, no matter how many times the binder is edited.

Each step feeds the next, and every recompile re-syncs the whole binder, so a change in step 2 never leaves step 4 out of date.

1

Create a Matter

Open a matter for the case, set the matter type, assign your team, and connect external document sources.

2

Upload & Organize

Upload PDFs and images and group them into exhibit sections: plaintiff, defendant, or witness by witness.

3

Configure & Compile

Set the Bates prefix, numbering scheme, and cover page, then compile. The system reflows pages, stamps numbers, and builds the exhibit index automatically.

4

Review & QC

Run a court-specific quality check, resolve any flagged issues, and mark exhibits confidential or attorneys'-eyes-only where required.

5

Export or Share

Download the final PDF binder, export a section-scoped ZIP, or generate a secure share link for opposing counsel or the court.
