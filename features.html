Exhibit Binder Studio was built around US exhibit and Bates conventions from the start. Here's what it does for you on every matter.

▦

Instant Exhibit Binders

Drag and drop exhibits into structured binders; automatic numbering, cover pages, and exhibit indexes are generated for you.

#

Bates Stamping

Sequential Bates numbers across an entire production in one action: configurable prefix, start number, and digit padding, with independent sequences per section.

✓

Court-Specific QC

Automated pre-filing quality checklists that catch structural and formatting issues before you file. (Detail below.)

▤

Soft Redaction

Mark, preview, then commit. Once applied, the underlying text, images, and vector graphics are permanently removed from the PDF, not merely covered. Irreversible by design.

⌕

OCR & Full-Text Search

Scanned PDFs and image documents are OCR-processed automatically, so every recognized word becomes searchable across the matter.

⤓

One-Click Export

Export a combined, bookmarked PDF binder or a section-scoped split ZIP, with privilege or attorneys'-eyes-only watermarks applied automatically.

Also included: Exhibit index export to CSV · custodian column on the index · comments pinned to a specific point on a specific page · full version history of every compiled binder · section-scoped share links · API keys · custom organization roles · a full audit log of every action.
